MyBarrie Transit provides trip planning and real-time transit information for Barrie Transit riders.

Independent app: MyBarrie Transit is independently operated by Mike McMike. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the City of Barrie or Barrie Transit.

For app support, contact Mike McMike at mybarrietransit@outlook.com or use the private feedback form in the app. For fares, schedules, transit service, or other Barrie Transit questions, contact Service Barrie.
