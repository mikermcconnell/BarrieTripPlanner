Delete your MyBarrie Transit account

Last updated: July 20, 2026

You can delete your account in the app. Service Barrie and the City of Barrie do not operate this app and cannot delete its accounts.

Delete in the app

  1. Open MyBarrie Transit and sign in.
  2. Open the Profile tab.
  3. Select Account.
  4. Select Delete account and confirm.

For security, you may be asked to sign out and sign in again before deletion. Successful in-app deletion removes the account immediately from the live authentication and account database.

Request deletion without the app

Email Mike McMike at mybarrietransit@outlook.com. State that you want to delete your MyBarrie Transit account and provide the email address used for the account. Do not send your password. The app operator may ask you to verify control of the account before processing the request.

Data deleted

  • Firebase sign-in account, email address, display name, and profile information.
  • Cloud-synced favorites, saved places and trips, trip history, route subscriptions, notification preferences, push tokens, and user settings stored under the account.
  • App account data held in the live user record and its subcollections.

Data not automatically deleted

  • Transit data and other caches stored on your device. Clear the cache in Settings or uninstall the app.
  • Feedback submitted through the private feedback form because feedback records do not contain your email address. Feedback is scheduled for deletion after no more than 365 days and can be removed earlier by authorized staff.
  • Pseudonymous rate-limit records, which are scheduled for deletion after no more than 30 days.
  • Limited logs, backups, or records that must be retained temporarily for security, continuity, or legal obligations.

Deletion cannot be undone. You can continue using core transit features without an account and may create a new account later.
